Loan Delinquency Risk Analysis

Financial institutions lose profitability when borrowers miss repayments — yet traditional monitoring is manual and reactive. This project analyzed 500 customer profiles to identify delinquency patterns, segment risk, and deliver two interactive Power BI dashboards for credit risk teams.

Dashboard 1 — Delinquency Analysis: Monitors repayment behavior over time, compares delinquency by location and credit card type, tracks monthly payment trends, and analyzes debt burden across income categories.

Dashboard 2 — Risk Assessment: Identifies high-risk customers by credit score vs missed payments, analyzes debt-to-income ratio by income group, and flags customers requiring review before loan approval.

Key findings: 80 of 500 customers identified as delinquent · Lower income segments carry highest debt-to-income ratio · Business credit card holders show highest delinquency rate · High-risk customers share pattern of low credit score + high outstanding balance + multiple missed payments.

500 customer profiles 80 delinquent customers flagged 2 Power BI dashboards Risk segmentation: Low · Medium · High

Healthcare Analytics Dashboard

Hospitals deal with thousands of patients daily — yet without clear visibility into patient flow, wait times, and satisfaction levels, resource allocation remains guesswork. I built this Power BI dashboard to give healthcare administrators a single view of all critical operational metrics across 9,216 patients.

Patient Flow & Volume — 71.33% of visits occur on weekdays vs 28.67% on weekends. Patient volume peaks between May and October and dips in November–December — giving administrators advance notice to plan staffing and resources accordingly. 58.59% of patients are walk-ins with no referral, indicating high direct footfall that departments must be prepared to absorb.

Department Referrals — General Practice leads referred departments with 1,840 patients, followed by Orthopedics at 995. Renal at 86 is the least visited — flagging either low demand or limited awareness that management can investigate.

Satisfaction & Wait Time — Average satisfaction score is 5.47 out of 10 with an average wait time of 35.26 minutes. The data suggests wait time is a key driver of lower scores. Native American patients in the Infancy group score as low as 1.00 — a critical gap requiring immediate attention.

Age & Gender — Adults dominate at 7,106 patients. Gender split is near equal — Male 51.05% vs Female 48.69% — confirming the hospital serves a balanced population across all groups.

Business Analysis Case Study

A real methodology document — from ambiguous client problem to root cause to dashboard.

CS-01
Logistics · Supply Chain

"Our deliveries are delayed" — from symptom to root cause.

Situation

A client reported on-time delivery dropped from 92% to 78%, complaints increased, and certain cities performed worse than others. No structured investigation existed.

Analysis

Mapped the full end-to-end process — Procurement → Inventory → Dispatch → Logistics Partner → Transit → Last Mile. Investigated 9 dimensions: Location, Supplier, Inventory, Shipment, Vehicle, Route, Partner, and Last Mile. Drill-down on Partner C revealed Kolkata as the problem city, large shipment batches as the cause, late vehicle departure due to batch consolidation delays as the root cause.

Recommendation

Root cause was vehicle allocation and batch-consolidation process — not Partner C itself. Fix: split large batches, set dispatch time SLAs, redesign consolidation logic. Expected OTD recovery to 90%+.

CS-02
BA Methodology · Root Cause

8-phase BA methodology built for any "something is wrong" problem.

Situation

Client stated "Partner C is the problem." A common mistake is to accept this and build a partner-performance dashboard immediately.

Analysis

Drilled down one level at a time — Partner C flagged → Kolkata performs worse than other cities → Large shipment batches perform worse → Large batches have longer consolidation time → Late vehicle departure → Long transit time → Late last-mile delivery (observed symptom).

Recommendation

Root cause hypothesis: Vehicle allocation and batch-consolidation process creates unnecessary waiting time. This is a specific, fixable process failure — not a vague partner complaint. Fix the consolidation logic and set dispatch time SLAs before evaluating the partner.

CS-03
Dashboard Design · BI

Dashboard structured around the investigation — not around available charts.

Situation

Once root cause is identified, the guiding dashboard question is not "which charts should I use?" but "what information does the client need to make a decision?"

Analysis

Proposed 5-page dashboard structure mapped directly to the investigation path — Page 1: Executive Overview · Page 2: Location Analysis (State → City → Zone → Area) · Page 3: Logistics Analysis (Partner → Vehicle → Route) · Page 4: Process Stage Timings · Page 5: Root Cause Breakdown by delay reason, stage, location, and partner.

Recommendation

Key KPIs to track: On-Time Delivery % · Average Delivery Time · Delayed Orders Count · Complaint Rate · Return Rate · Partner SLA Adherence · Stage-wise Average Time · Batch Consolidation Wait Time · Last Mile Delivery Success Rate.
